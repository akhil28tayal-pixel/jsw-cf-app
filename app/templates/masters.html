SAP Stock Adjustment — permanent SAP vs physical gaps

For a discrepancy that no entry will ever close — material short at a godown handover, say. SAP keeps carrying stock that physically isn't there, so the shortage is subtracted from the SAP side every time the Stock page reconciles. Enter bags as a positive number when SAP over-states; a negative number if SAP under-states. Each handover gets its own row, and they are summed per product.
