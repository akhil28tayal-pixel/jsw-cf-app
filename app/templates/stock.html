SAP vs Physical Reconciliation
Upload SAP stock statement → Excel

SAP drops stock when an invoice is raised, not when the truck leaves — so the two figures differ for reasons that are expected. Advance (billed, not yet dispatched) is still in the godown, so it is added to SAP. Hold (dispatched, not yet billed) has gone, so it is subtracted. Shortage is a permanent gap recorded under Masters, e.g. material short at a godown handover, and is subtracted too.
SAP + Advance − Hold − Shortage should equal physical stock. Anything in the Difference column is a real problem — a missed dispatch entry, a billing row not imported, or genuine stock loss.
